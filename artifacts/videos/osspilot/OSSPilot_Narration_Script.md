Open source is full of opportunities. But finding the right issue, understanding unfamiliar codebases, and making meaningful contributions takes time.

Meet OSSPilot - your AI-powered, local-first open-source contribution agent.

Connected through GitHub's Octokit API, OSSPilot discovers repositories, analyzes issues, inspects codebases, and identifies meaningful contribution opportunities.

Built with TypeScript, Bun, OpenTUI and SQLite, it combines explainable repository intelligence with an extensible AI-provider architecture.

After human approval, OSSPilot creates an isolated Git worktree and delegates implementation to OpenCode using your configured AI provider.

It then independently reviews the changes, runs verification, and prepares your contribution for review.

OSSPilot. Discover. Understand. Contribute.
